description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Annoyed by leftover baseboards and wall trims ruining your base renovations?
description=Tired of seeing decorative wall elements that cannot be removed or dismantled in vanilla without destroy the entire wall?
description=Thanks to this mod, you can finally tear down those unwanted wall trims and clean up your interior design!
description=
description=[h2]How does it work?[/h2][list]
description=[*] Adds various wall decoration sprites (such as baseboards and wall trims) to the list of disassemblable objects.
description=[*] Features a custom disassembly system tailored for this mod, while still seamlessly integrating into the vanilla disassembly interaction menu.
description=[*] [b]How to use:[/b] To start dismantling, right-click a wall trim and select the disassembly option from the context menu. Once the first piece is dismantled, the disassembly target cursor automatically activates, allowing you to chain-dismantle surrounding trims without reopening the menu!
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Limitations[/h2][list]
description=[*] Due to how Project Zomboid handles tile rendering, this mod specifically targets "attached" sprites.
description=[*] Some baseboards or trims in the game are hardcoded directly onto full wall tiles rather than attached sprites. As a result, these specific walls cannot have their trims removed separately without destroying the entire wall itself.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Planned features[/h2][list]
description=[*] Support for even more wall trim and decorative sprites.
description=Finding every single corresponding sprite in the game files is quite a puzzle! While a vast majority are already covered, some might still be missing.
description=[b]Found an unsupported trim?[/b] Feel free to let me know in the comments section! Please provide the [b]in-game coordinates (X, Y, Z)[/b] of the location where you found it so I can add it in future updates.
description=[/list]
